# Bible Daily — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 13 November 2026_

Bible Daily ("the app") is published by Robert Langat. This policy explains what data the app handles.

## Data that stays on your device

- Your bookmarks, highlights, notes, reading position and settings (text size, theme, reminder time) are stored **only on your device**. They are never uploaded to us. We have no servers and no user accounts.
- The Bible text is bundled with the app. Reading and searching work without an internet connection.
- If Android backup is enabled on your phone, Android may include the app's data in your personal Google device backup. Google handles that backup, not us.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner and occasional full-screen (interstitial) ads. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

- your device's advertising ID, IP address, and general device and app information
- ad interactions (for example, ad views and taps), crash and performance diagnostics

Google uses this data under its own policies. See ["How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services"](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

- Where the law requires it (for example, in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings › Ad privacy choices** when that option is shown.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your phone's **Settings › Google › Ads** (or **Settings › Privacy › Ads**).

## Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No data is sent anywhere for this.

## Children

The app is a general-audience Bible reader and is not directed at children under 13.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, use the developer contact email shown on the app's store listing.
